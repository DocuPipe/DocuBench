Florida homeowners policy declarations page. Extract dollar amounts as plain numbers without the $ sign or thousands separators. Dates appear as M/D/YY; normalize to YYYY-MM-DD. The coverages array holds the Section I property coverages plus Section II liability coverages (Coverage A through F), each with its letter, name and limit of liability. The hurricane deductible is stated as a percent of Coverage A and reconciles: hurricaneDeductibleAmount equals hurricaneDeductiblePercentOfCoverageA percent of the Coverage A limit. Extract hurricanePremium, nonHurricanePremium and totalAnnualPremium exactly as printed; the component premiums may not sum to the total on the document, so do not recompute or adjust them.
