Bilingual (English + Arabic) freight forwarding TAX INVOICE (فاتورة ضريبية). Header fields are printed in English with Arabic equivalents; capture the English values. Dates are in DD-MON-YY format (e.g. 05-JAN-19); record exactly as printed. The charge table has one row per charge with columns: Charge Description, Curr (currency), Rate Per Unit, Unit, Curr. Amount, ROE (rate of exchange), Total Price excl. VAT, VAT%, VAT Amount, Total. Capture every charge row. The Total line gives the summed totalExclVat, totalVat and grandTotal in AED; VAT is 5%. Amounts are numbers as printed without thousands separators. Charge descriptions are short labels and may be captured.
