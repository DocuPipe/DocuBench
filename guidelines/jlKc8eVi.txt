Japanese delivery-note-and-invoice (納品書兼御請求書). The seller is shown in the top-right header; the buyer (御中) and buyer address (御住所) are on the left. Dates use Japanese era format (平成 / Heisei); record the issue date (top-right) exactly as printed. Each row of the item table has columns: 商品名 (product name), 商品コード (product code), 数量 (quantity), 単価 (unit price), 値引き (discount percentage), 小計 (line subtotal), 適用 (remark). Capture every item row. 合計 is the grand total in yen at the bottom right; record it as a number without the 円 suffix or thousands separators. Field names and descriptions are in English; values stay in the original Japanese script and numbers as printed.
