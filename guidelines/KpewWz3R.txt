Uniform Residential Appraisal Report (Fannie Mae form 1004 / FHA). Extract the structured subject-property fields and the sales-comparison grid. Do NOT extract the prose sections: scope of work, definition of market value, statement of assumptions, appraiser/supervisory certifications, or attached addenda narrative. Dollar amounts are plain numbers without $ or separators. Dates with masked digits (e.g. 05/02/201X) cannot be normalized, so leave the masked date out rather than guessing. The comparables array is the flattened sales-comparison grid: one entry per comparable sale that has a sale price, drawn from both the first grid (comps 1-3) and the continuation grid (comps 4-5). Each comparable carries its address, sale price, total net adjustment, and the adjusted sale price; do not nest the individual line-item adjustments. netAdjustmentTotal is the signed net adjustment (negative if the minus box is checked). Reconciliation: a comparable's adjustedSalePrice equals its salePrice plus netAdjustmentTotal.
